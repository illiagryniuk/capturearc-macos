# CaptureArc brand system

## Meaning

The mark combines the three product ideas that should remain recognizable at menu-bar size:

- a screenshot card;
- the curved handoff path from the lower-right edge;
- the cyan-to-indigo-to-violet rim around the MacBook notch.

The result avoids generic camera apertures and checkmarks, which are crowded categories in screenshot utilities.

## Palette

- Night: `#090B1A`
- Cyan: `#21D1ED`
- Indigo: `#6466F2`
- Violet: `#7D3BEB`
- Card: `#EDF1FF`

## Usage

- Use `CaptureArc-AppStore-1024.png` as the App Store source icon.
- Use `CaptureArc-mark.svg` for scalable marketing art.
- Use `CaptureArc-wordmark.svg` on the support and privacy pages.
- Do not add text inside the app icon.
- Keep at least 12% clear space around the standalone mark.
- On dark backgrounds, use the full-color mark. On light backgrounds, use the wordmark as supplied.
