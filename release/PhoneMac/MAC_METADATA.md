## Description
CaptureArc keeps useful things within reach at the top of your Mac screen.

See recent screenshots and screen recordings in a visual shelf. Keep folders in persistent tabs. With the separate CaptureArc Sender app for iPhone, send selected text, photos, links, and files directly to your Mac over Wi-Fi.

FROM IPHONE TO YOUR WORK
• Pair once by scanning a QR code in CaptureArc Sender.
• Use the iPhone Share menu to send items from other apps.
• See photos and text as cards, ready to drag into another app.
• Received text is copied automatically, ready for Command-V.
• Pairing resumes when CaptureArc reopens, until you choose Stop Receiving.
• Keep up to 20 received items until you remove them.

YOUR CAPTURE SHELF
• Reach recent screenshots and screen recordings at the top of your screen.
• Copy, share, reveal, or drag captures into your workflow.
• Keep favorite folders available in tabs across app restarts.
• Choose optional capture retention. Eligible captures go to the macOS Trash.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, ads, analytics, or CaptureArc cloud service. Transfers use an encrypted local connection. You choose what to send and which folders the Mac app may access.

Requires macOS 13 or later. iPhone sharing requires the separate CaptureArc Sender app on iOS 17 or later, both devices on the same Wi-Fi network, and CaptureArc open on the Mac. Send up to 5 items at a time: photos/files up to 10 MB each, text up to 1 MB. Notes' top Share button shares the whole note; use the selection menu or copy selected text into Sender to send only part of a note.

## What's New
Send text, photos, links, and files from your iPhone with the separate CaptureArc Sender app. Received items appear as visual cards, ready to drag and drop. Text is copied automatically for immediate pasting. Pairing and received cards stay available across app restarts, and you can stop receiving or remove cards whenever you choose.
