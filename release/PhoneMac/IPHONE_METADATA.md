## Description
CaptureArc Sender is the iPhone companion to CaptureArc for Mac.

Send selected text, photos, web links, and files straight to your Mac over the same Wi-Fi network. No account or cloud upload is required.

PAIR ONCE
Open From iPhone in CaptureArc on your Mac, choose Pair iPhone, and scan the QR code in Sender. Pairing remains saved until you stop receiving on the Mac. Your Mac needs to be awake with CaptureArc open to receive.

SHARE FROM YOUR APPS
Choose text, photos, or a link, then open Share and choose CaptureArc. If it is hidden, swipe to More and add it to your favorites. Sender also includes a text field and a file picker.

READY ON YOUR MAC
Photos and text arrive as visual cards. Drag a card into another app. Received text is automatically copied on the Mac so you can paste it immediately with Command-V. Sender reports success only after the Mac acknowledges receipt.

SELECT ONLY WHAT YOU NEED
To send part of a note, use Share from the text selection menu. If your source app does not offer that action, copy the selection and paste it into Sender. Notes' top Share button sends the whole note.

PRIVATE AND DIRECT
No ads, tracking, account, or CaptureArc cloud service. Your chosen items travel over an encrypted local Wi-Fi connection. The pairing secret is stored in the devices' Keychains. Camera access is used only to scan the Mac's QR code.

Requires CaptureArc 1.2 or later on a Mac running macOS 13 or later. Both devices must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network. Supports up to 5 items per share: photos/files up to 10 MB each and text up to 1 MB. Networks that isolate devices can prevent discovery. This app sends to a Mac; it does not receive transfers from a Mac.
