Privacy Policy

CaptureArc for Mac and CaptureArc Sender for iPhone keep your work on your devices.

Your devices, your content

Neither app collects data for the developer, sells data, tracks you, or uses advertising or analytics services. There is no CaptureArc account or cloud service. Items you choose to send travel directly from iPhone to your paired Mac over encrypted local Wi-Fi.

Files and shared items

The Mac app reads captures and file listings from folders you authorize with the standard macOS folder picker. App-scoped bookmarks restore that access. Sender reads only the items you select through the iPhone Share menu or file picker, or the text you enter into Sender. It does not scan your photo library or read your clipboard in the background.

Pairing and network access

A QR code pairs your devices using a random secret stored in their Keychains. Keep that code private. Both devices need the same Wi-Fi network. Receiving resumes when you reopen CaptureArc on the Mac until you choose Stop Receiving. Stop Receiving disables the pairing; Forget Mac clears the phone's saved secret. No shared content or pairing secret is sent to the developer.

Clipboard and other apps

Successfully received text replaces the Mac clipboard so you can paste it immediately. Photos and links do not replace it automatically. Other apps and operating-system clipboard features may access or sync copied text according to their settings. Opening links or dragging items into other apps makes them subject to those apps' privacy practices.

Permissions

The iPhone app requests Camera access only to scan a pairing QR; manual code entry works without it. Local Network access enables direct transfer. Neither app requests Contacts, Location, Microphone, or tracking permission. CaptureArc for Mac does not capture your screen or require Screen Recording, Accessibility, Input Monitoring, or Full Disk Access.

Storage and deletion

The Mac keeps up to 20 received cards in private app storage across restarts. Removing a card or clearing the received shelf deletes that Mac copy, including the local text or file. Originals on iPhone remain in their source app. Removing a card does not clear text already copied to the clipboard. Sender stores its pairing secret, but does not keep a history of transferred content. The source app or operating system may retain originals, temporary provider files, or backups.

Existing screenshots and files remain under your control. Optional capture cleanup moves eligible new captures to the macOS Trash. It does not automatically delete received iPhone cards.

Diagnostics

Content-free transfer-stage messages may be written to the local operating-system log. They contain no shared contents, filenames, pairing codes, or peer addresses and are not uploaded by CaptureArc. Apple may process App Store or device diagnostics under your Apple settings and its own policies.

Changes

We update this policy and the App Store disclosures when privacy practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a private-data-free privacy request. Do not include screenshots or other sensitive files in a public issue.
