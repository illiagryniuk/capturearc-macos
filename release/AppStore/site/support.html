CaptureArc Support

CaptureArc watches one folder you authorize and brings new captures to a shelf at the top of your screen.

First-time setup

1. Open CaptureArc and choose Authorize Capture Folder….
2. Press Shift + Command + 5 and open Options.
3. Under Save to, choose Other Location… and select the same folder.
4. Take a screenshot. Hover over or click the colored rim around the notch to open the shelf.

If a capture does not appear

  • Confirm macOS Screenshot and CaptureArc use the same folder.
  • Open CaptureArc Settings and choose Rescan Now.
  • If the folder was moved or renamed, choose Change Capture Folder… and authorize it again.
  • Clipboard-only screenshots do not create a file and cannot appear in the shelf.

Contact

If these steps do not help, open a support request. Do not attach private screenshots; describe the behavior and your macOS version instead.
